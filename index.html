Inside the shop

Pie on the counter, jam on the shelf.

Fresh bread, pies and sweet things to take home. There's also The Rehatched Nook, the shop's new vintage and repurposed corner, worth a wander after lunch.

The shop

A little place with a lot of table manners.

On the Square is a tiny, tidy bakery and deli in the middle of Eden. There are five tables, you order at the counter yourself, and someone friendly always notices when you walk in.

Everything leans homemade, from the bread to the pies, and people drive in from all over the Concho Valley to eat it. Linger a while. The park is right across the street.
